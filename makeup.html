Saree Drapping


The Foundation: Put on the heels you plan to wear to ensure the correct length. Tie your petticoat or shapewear securely at the navel. and a long-wear primer for a smooth, lasting base.
The First Tuck: Take the plain inner end of the saree and start tucking it into your petticoat. Ensure it lies perfectly flat to avoid bulky bulges. Wrap it around your waist once..
Make the Center Pleats: With the remaining fabric, gather the cloth into neat, even pleats. Tuck these centered pleats slightly to the left of your navel and pin them securely to your petticoat.
